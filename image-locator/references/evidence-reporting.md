# Evidence evaluation and reporting

## Candidate record

Maintain one row per candidate camera area with: (1) observed pixels and any untrusted tag claim; (2) proposed feature interpretation, marking cues that only selected a search region; (3) original independent source URL and source or capture date; (4) predicted versus observed road shape, landmark order, bearing, occlusion, skyline or other geometry; (5) supporting and conflicting facts; (6) remaining uncertainty and resulting precision. Log transformations, calibration and calculator inputs. Distinguish a source that **identifies a subject** from one that constrains the **camera**. Reposts of a caption, copied coordinates and derivative map labels share one evidence lineage. The [Berkeley Protocol's verification and reporting sections](https://humanrights.berkeley.edu/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/Berkeley-Protocol.pdf) call for source assessment, corroboration and transparent limits.

Require each leading camera candidate to survive an **independent viewpoint check**: use a mapped fixed feature excluded from candidate selection, another imagery source or a held-out image feature to predict what that viewpoint should show. Deliberately search for the strongest contradictory geometry, including reversed landmark order, an incompatible bend, a blocked sightline or a missing skyline feature. Record the test even when it finds no contradiction. Check a homography on unused points from its plane; any pose derived from it or from PnP needs held-out reprojection results and sensitivity to calibration and mapped heights before it supports point precision. Challenge each leader with the strongest plausible alternative and with any contrary metadata or imagery date. Treat a signed provenance claim as a claim, and a calculated solar or AIS agreement as conditional on its inputs. Keep unresolved contradictions visible; explain whether benign processing, uncertain time, changed scenery or deliberate false metadata could account for them. Confidence should name the supported level (region, district, street side, building, camera point) and why, rather than imply a numerical probability without calibration.

## Final answer

State the supported camera area and a bounded uncertainty extent. Give an exact coordinate only when independent geometry or direct verifiable capture evidence isolates the camera position. Otherwise use a representative **approximate** pin inside the supported building, block or polygon, round it to area-scale precision, and say that the pin is not the shutter position. List material alternative areas and what evidence would separate them. Label any photographed landmark coordinate as the subject's location. Link the few original sources that carry the conclusion, plus the image observation and any timing assumptions. Decimal places and a map marker do not improve underlying accuracy. If useful, [Google Maps documents coordinate search URLs](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/urls/get-started): `https://www.google.com/maps/search/?api=1&query=LAT%2CLON`.
